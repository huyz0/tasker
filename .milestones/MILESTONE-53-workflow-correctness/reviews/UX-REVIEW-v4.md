# M53-T04 pending work and explicit history

Pending work is immediately actionable; newer decisions cannot crowd it out of a
mixed preview. History is a quieter native disclosure with independent pages and
loaded/total counts. Organization queues reach other projects and later pages.
Local Retry preserves earlier forms and typing, while concurrent decisions leave
a clear explanation outside a row that may disappear after reconciliation.

UX-v3 findings are corrected. Known access loss disables retained controls until
fresh success, including remount/scope-return. A late old-scope callback cannot
remove the current error. Visible organization queues have bounded off-feed
refresh without another stream. Independent human-workflow re-review approves.

Forty-nine focused cases, two actual browser journeys,72 settled rendered
light/dark desktop/mobile axe audits and18 overflow checks pass. The first dark
browser scan found a1.75:1 Delete-control contrast failure; the existing semantic
subtle pair fixes it without weakening any audit rule. The actual mobile capture
was visually inspected for readable alert, hierarchy, counts, disclosure and
single task-dialog scroll owner. All35 local checks pass.
